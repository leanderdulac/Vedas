=== KATHA UPANISHAD ===
Naciketas and Yama. Death grants three boons.

On the Self:
The knowing Self is not born; It does not die. It did not spring from anything, and nothing sprang from It. Birthless, eternal, everlasting and ancient, It is not slain when the body is slain.

Smaller than the small, greater than the great, the Self is set in the heart of every creature. The unstriving man beholds Him, freed from sorrow, through purity of mind and senses.

The sharp edge of a razor is difficult to pass over; thus the wise say the path to the Self is hard.

Arise, awake, approach the great and learn. Like the sharp edge of a razor is that path, so the wise say—hard to tread and difficult to cross.

Two enter the cave of the heart: the knowing and the unknowing, both dwelling in the highest Brahman. Of these, the knowers of Brahman speak as light and shade.

Yama teaches: the good is one thing, the pleasant another. Both bind a man; the wise chooses the good; the fool chooses the pleasant.

=== MUNDAKA UPANISHAD ===
Two kinds of knowledge must be known—the higher and the lower. The lower is the Rig Veda, Yajur Veda, Sama Veda, Atharva Veda, phonetics, ritual, grammar, etymology, metre and astronomy. The higher is that by which the Imperishable is apprehended.

As from a blazing fire thousands of sparks similar to it issue forth, so from the Imperishable, my friend, various beings come forth and return thither again.

Brahman, in truth, is this immortal. In front is Brahman, behind is Brahman, to the right and to the left. It spreads forth below and above. Brahman, indeed, is this universe.

He who knows that supreme Brahman becomes Brahman indeed. In his line none is born who knows not Brahman. He overcomes grief, overcomes sin, and being free from the knots of the heart, becomes immortal.

=== CHANDOGYA UPANISHAD (selected) ===
Tat tvam asi — That thou art.
Uddalaka teaches Svetaketu: That which is the subtle essence, this whole world has for its self. That is the true. That is the Self. That thou art, Svetaketu.

In the beginning this was Being alone, one only without a second. Some say in the beginning this was non-being alone. But how could being be born from non-being? On the contrary, in the beginning this was Being alone, one only without a second.

As bees make honey by collecting juices of different trees and reduce them into one essence, and as these juices have no discrimination so as to say I am the juice of this tree or that, even so, all these creatures when they reach Being know not that they have reached Being.

=== BRIHADARANYAKA UPANISHAD (selected) ===
Aum. The face of truth is covered with a golden disc. Unveil it, O Pushan, so that I who love the truth may see.

Neti neti — not this, not this. The Self is not this, not that. It is unseizable, for it cannot be seized; indestructible, for it cannot be destroyed; unattached, for it does not attach itself; unbound; it does not tremble; it is not injured.

As a man acts, as he behaves, so does he become. The doer of good becomes good; the doer of evil becomes evil. By pure actions he becomes pure; by evil actions, evil.

=== TAITTIRIYA UPANISHAD (selected) ===
From that Brahman, which is the Self, was produced space. From space, air; from air, fire; from fire, water; from water, earth; from earth, herbs; from herbs, food; from food, man.

Food is Brahman; from food beings are born; by food, when born, they grow. Food is eaten and food eats things. Therefore it is called food.

There is a sheath of food, of vital breath, of mind, of understanding, and of bliss. The knower of Brahman attains the Supreme.

=== MANDUKYA UPANISHAD ===
Aum, this syllable is all this. All that is past, present and future is indeed Aum. And whatever else there is beyond the threefold time, that also is truly Aum.

All this is Brahman. This Self is Brahman. This same Self has four quarters: the waking (Vaishvanara), the dream (Taijasa), the deep sleep (Prajna), and the Fourth (Turiya).

Turiya is not that which is conscious of the inner world, nor that which is conscious of the outer world, nor that which is conscious of both, nor that which is a mass of consciousness, nor that which is simple consciousness, nor that which is unconscious. It is unseen, unrelated, incomprehensible, uninferable, unthinkable, indescribable; the essence of the consciousness of the one Self; the cessation of the phenomenal world; quiescent, auspicious, non-dual. That is the Self; that is to be known.

=== SHVETASHVATARA UPANISHAD (selected) ===
He who is one, the creator of the creator, who creates many forms by his own power, in whom the universe dissolves at the end—may that Lord, the bestower of blessings, endow us with good thoughts.

The one God, hidden in all beings, all-pervading, the inner Self of all, supervising all actions, dwelling in all beings, the witness, the knower, the only one, free from qualities.
